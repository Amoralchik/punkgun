MOVEMENT & COMBAT

WASD move · MOUSE aim · LMB strike/shoot · R reload · RMB throw weapon · SPACE dash · E execute stunned enemies

Everyone dies in one hit. Armor plates from cyberware absorb one hit each and give a short grace window. Dash through a room, chain kills inside the combo window, and finish stunned targets with E. A Z quickhack needs an Intrusion Deck and a visible target.

SHIELD units block the front — flank, backstab, or throw. SNIPERS paint a red laser before firing. RUSHERS close fast with melee. GRENADIERS lob red fused grenades and keep their distance. BLADES are unarmed cyberpsychos that close faster than anything else. NETRUNNERS channel a visible hack that scrambles the HUD and locks active cyberware; break sight or kill them first. OPERATORS deploy turrets and carry a sidearm.

EXPLOSIVE BARRELS chain-react. Shoot one beside a group, or throw a weapon into it. SECURITY CAMERAS sweep corridors and alert the sector; shoot them out. TURRETS paint a red line before firing — break line of sight or destroy them. LASER GRIDS pulse across corridors; cross on the dark phase or shoot the emitter.

GLASS blocks movement but not sight. Shoot a pane to open a route. STEAM VENTS pulse sight-blocking clouds; use them or wait out the cycle. HOUNDS leap; MEDICS restore ally plates; kill the medic first.

CONTRACTS

PURGE kill every hostile · HIT kill the marked target · RECOVER extract caches · SABOTAGE plant taps on marked terminals · EXTRACTION secure the case and survive the converge · HUNT chase a fleeing mark · DEFENSE hold the zone for 45 seconds. Exit opens when the objective is done.

CHAPTERS street, Sublevel, and Spire each gate on three clears and a boss. Four bosses: the augmented psycho, GRIN, DEADWEIGHT, and the Board. The finale usually ends in one of three choices; the Sublevel REMEMBER choice opens a fourth.

BOSSES break armor plates; every shattered plate summons adds and releases a radial shockwave. From phase 2 they telegraph a charge — break line of sight or sidestep. After a charge or plate break the magenta core is exposed; a hit in that window removes two plates.

SAFEHOUSE stations: CONTRACTS, MARKET, SUPPLIES, WORKBENCH, CLINIC, ARCHIVE, FABRICATOR, OPERATOR, SANCTUARY, and DEPLOY. TAB opens the terminal overview and the attaché case. SANCTUARY upgrades change the room and your run; the MARKET sells insurance and opens the EXCHANGE. The deploy pad always asks which contract to run before launch.

LOOT & MONEY

E grabs caches, weapons, and slams doors. Loot becomes contraband only on a clean exit; sell it in the safehouse. Contract pay + time bonus are banked on clear.

Q uses your packed supply: stim armor, smoke, flashbang, or frag grenade.

COMPONENTS drop from kills; the FABRICATOR turns them into ammo, attachments, stims, and weapons that stow in the attaché case.

AUGMENTS & XP

Clears grant XP and levels. Levels give perk points for the clinic; credits buy permanent cyberware. Armor, speed, spread, and enemy vision all come from your build.

ACTIVE 'WARE — SHIFT Sandevistan slows the world; C Optical Camo breaks line of sight; X Microgenerator EMP; V Berserk; G Overclock; H Intrusion Deck. Second Heart, Biomonitor, Mantis Blades, and Gorilla Arms are passive installs.

NEURAL LOAD — every implant consumes humanity. Above 60% strain the ripperdoc may reject an install; above 85% armor and vision suffer. BODY and level raise capacity.

STEALTH & HEAT

Vision cones show enemy sight. Approach from behind and press E for a silent takedown. Gunfire raises heat; at high heat, reinforcements breach the sector.

ACCESSIBILITY — pause → settings: reduced motion, colorblind palettes, subtitles, event log, and WASD/ESDF/ARROWS control presets.

DEATH & RETRY

On death, R retries the same contract with fists; SPACE returns to the safehouse. A clear extracts whatever weapon you are holding and pays out.
